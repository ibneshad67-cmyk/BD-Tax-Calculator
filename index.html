Other Income Sources
AY 2027-28
1Business / Professional Income
Fully taxable. The full amount is added to your taxable income and taxed under progressive slabs.
2Rental Income
A standard 30% deduction is applied for repair & maintenance. Only 70% of rental income is added to taxable income.
3Dividend Income
Subject to Final Tax at 15%. This amount is NOT added to progressive tax slabs. A separate dividend tax will be shown in results.
4Gross Agricultural Income
First Tk. 5,00,000 is exempt. Only the amount above Tk. 5,00,000 is added to taxable income.
5Other Financial Assets Income
Bank interest, FDR interest, etc. These are subject to Final Tax (already deducted at source). Shown separately in results — not added to progressive slabs.
6Tax Exempted Income
For reference only — e.g. remittance income. This amount is completely excluded from tax calculations.
